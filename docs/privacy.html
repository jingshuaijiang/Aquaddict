Aquaddict 隐私政策
Privacy Policy

最后更新:2026 年 8 月 · Last updated: August 2026

一句话总结 · In one sentence

Aquaddict 的所有数据只保存在你的手机上。没有账号、没有服务器、没有统计分析、没有广告,我们不收集、不上传、也看不到你的任何数据。

Everything Aquaddict stores lives on your phone. No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads — we do not collect, upload, or ever see any of your data.

本地存储的数据 · Data stored on your device

  • 从潜水电脑下载的潜水日志(含深度曲线和潜水电脑记录的 GPS 坐标)
    Dive logs downloaded from your dive computer, including depth profiles and GPS positions the computer recorded
  • 你添加的潜点、潜伴、照片、物种记录、装备与配重设置
    Dive sites, buddies, photos, species, gear and weighting settings you add

这些数据全部保存在应用的本地沙盒中,卸载应用即彻底删除。
All of it lives in the app's local sandbox; uninstalling the app deletes it completely.

系统权限 · System permissions

  • 蓝牙:仅用于连接你的 Shearwater 潜水电脑下载日志。
    Bluetooth — only to talk to your Shearwater dive computer.
  • 相机 / 照片:仅在你主动为潜水添加照片时使用,照片保存在本地。
    Camera / Photos — only when you attach photos to a dive; they stay on the device.
  • 通讯录:仅在你主动从通讯录选择潜伴时读取所选联系人,保存在本地。
    Contacts — only when you pick a buddy from your contacts; the selection stays on the device.

唯一的网络请求 · The only network requests

当你在潜点页面搜索附近潜点时,应用会把该潜水的坐标发送给 Apple 地图和 OpenStreetMap(Nominatim)以返回地名结果。这是应用唯一的联网行为,不附带任何身份信息,且只在你主动搜索时发生。

When you search for nearby dive sites, the app sends that dive's coordinates to Apple Maps and OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) to fetch place names. This is the app's only network activity, carries no identity, and happens only when you tap search.
